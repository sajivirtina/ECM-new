1. Who we are

ElderCareMatters.com is operated by ElderCare Matters, LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("ElderCareMatters," "we," "us"). This policy covers families and visitors, providers who list with us, and readers of our guides. Effective date: October 15, 2026.

2. Information we collect

From families

  • Contact details: first and last name, email, phone number.
  • Location: the city, state or ZIP code where care is needed.
  • Care details: care category, who needs care (parent, spouse, grandparent, yourself, someone else), how soon, and any notes you choose to write.
  • Your provider selections: the up to 10 providers you pick.
  • Contact preferences: email and phone. We do not send text messages. See Section 6.
  • Consent records: the date, time, form text, IP address and provider list from your submission.

From providers

  • Business details: name, address, phone, email, service categories, cities served, credentials and license information.
  • Content you post: descriptions, photos, articles, team bios.
  • Billing: your plan, payment method and payment history. Card numbers go to our payment processor. We do not store them.
  • Sign-in through a third-party service such as Google, LinkedIn or Facebook, where we offer it and you use it. We receive your name and email from that service, never your password.

Collected automatically

  • IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed.
  • Approximate location, used to show providers near you.
  • Cookies and similar tools. See Section 7.

4. How we share information

For families, sharing follows one path:

  • Every provider you picked sees your request right away with your name, phone, email and notes hidden. They see the care type, city, timing and a short summary of the need, with identifying details removed.
  • A provider you picked may request your contact details during its access window. When it does, it receives your name, phone, email and notes.
  • Our provider terms limit every provider to one use: responding to your request through the channels you chose. Providers may not sell your details or add you to marketing lists.
  • If no listed provider serves your category and city, your request goes to the ElderCareMatters team, who may contact you to help.

Expedited matching. If you choose expedited matching and the providers you picked have not responded, typically within 72 hours and sometimes sooner, we share your masked request with additional verified providers in your area, at your direction. They see the same masked summary your picked providers saw, and they receive your contact details exactly the way your picked providers do: only by requesting them. Your name, phone, email and notes are never shown to any provider that has not requested them through this process. If you skip expedited matching, your request never goes beyond your picks.

You choose expedited matching on the request form. It is optional, and your request never goes beyond your picks unless you select it.

Vendors and other disclosures. Vendors process data for us under contract, only to provide their service: payments (Stripe), email delivery (Brevo or Zoho), analytics (Google) and website hosting. We may also disclose information to comply with law, to protect rights and safety, or in a merger or sale of the business, with notice.

About "sales" under state privacy laws. Some states define a sale as any disclosure of personal data for value. We disclose your information to providers only because you selected them and directed us to share it. State privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act, generally exclude disclosures made at the consumer's direction from the definition of a sale. We never sell family information to anyone, and we never share family data for advertising. For providers and provider prospects, we may advertise our own services through ad platforms, which some state laws treat as "sharing" or "targeted advertising." Providers can opt out anytime through the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link, by emailing Rights@ElderCareMatters.com, or with a Global Privacy Control signal, which we honor.

5. Health information

Your category choice or notes can reveal health information, for example a dementia diagnosis. We treat that as sensitive data:

  • We collect it only because you provide it to find care.
  • We share it only as Section 4 describes, with the consent you give when you submit.
  • Masked requests carry a short summary of the care need, with names, contact details and other identifying information stripped out. Your full notes go only to a provider who asks to help.
  • You can withdraw consent anytime at Help@ElderCareMatters.com. We stop new disclosures and direct your picked providers to stop contact.

ElderCareMatters is not a health care provider, and this site is not covered by HIPAA. Share only what providers need to help you.

Washington and Nevada residents have added rights over consumer health data, including access, withdrawal of consent, deletion and a list of recipients. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice explains them. To exercise them, email Rights@ElderCareMatters.com.

6. Calls and email

When you submit a request you give us a phone number and an email address. We release those details to a provider only when that provider asks for them, and only to providers your request was sent to. Those providers may then call you or email you about your request.

We do not send text messages. Nothing on this site gives us or any provider permission to text you. If you ever receive a text about an ElderCareMatters request, tell us at Help@ElderCareMatters.com.

You can stop all contact at any time. Every email we send you carries a one-click link that stops contact about that request. It takes effect immediately, we stop emailing you about it, and we tell every provider that received your details to stop contacting you. You can also email Help@ElderCareMatters.com. We keep a record of your consent, including which providers you selected and the exact words you agreed to.

Providers are independent businesses. If a provider contacts you in a way you did not agree to, that is the provider's own conduct. Tell us, and we will take it up with them and can remove them from the directory.

7. Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics (through Site Kit), a sharing widget and a feedback widget. These set cookies and collect usage data. You can limit cookies in your browser settings, use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and send a Global Privacy Control signal, which we honor where required. Family pages, including Find Care, the category pages and the request flow, never carry advertising or retargeting cookies. Provider-facing pages, such as For Providers and Plans & Pricing, may set advertising cookies used only to market our own services to providers. Opt out through the Do Not Sell or Share link or a Global Privacy Control signal.

8. Your rights

Depending on your state (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia), you may have the right to:

  • Know and get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete it.
  • Opt out of sales, targeted advertising and certain profiling.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (California).

Email Rights@ElderCareMatters.com. Include your full name, the email and phone number you used on the site, your state, and which right you are exercising. We verify requests by matching those details and may send a confirmation code. Authorized agents may submit requests where state law allows. We never treat anyone differently for exercising rights. If we decline a request, reply "appeal" and we will review it within the period your state's law sets. If we still decline, you may contact your state attorney general.
